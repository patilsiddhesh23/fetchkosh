FetchKosh Privacy Policy

Last updated: 20 November 2026

In short

  • FetchKosh has no account and no sign-in, and we don't run any server that the apps report to.
  • We don't collect, sell or share your personal information. There is no analytics or tracking.
  • Your downloads, settings and the devices you allow stay on your own phone, TV and PC.
  • FetchKosh connects only to websites you choose, a few named services (listed below) when you use a feature that needs them, and your own devices on your home Wi-Fi.

This policy covers FetchKosh for Android phones, FetchKosh for Android TV, FetchKosh for Windows, the FetchKosh browser extension, and this website (fetchkosh.pages.dev). "We" means the developer of FetchKosh.

What we collect

Nothing. FetchKosh does not send us your files, your downloads, the links you use, your device details or any other personal information. There is no analytics, crash reporting or tracking library in the apps.

Like any app or browser, FetchKosh has to connect to the internet to download. The websites and services it connects to see the normal information any connection carries, such as your IP address. Each one is listed in the sections below.

Android phone app

What stays on your phone

  • The files you download, saved in Download/FetchKosh.
  • Your download list and your settings (for example dark or light look, Wi-Fi only, the daily mobile data limit, night downloads).
  • How much Wi-Fi and mobile data FetchKosh used today, for the daily limit.
  • The TVs and PCs you allowed to connect: their names and a pairing key.
  • For Watch on TV: a link to each file you chose to share. The files themselves are not copied or uploaded.

Downloads and the built-in browser

FetchKosh connects to the websites you choose: links you paste, share to it or copy, and pages you open in its built-in browser. It can use Wi-Fi and mobile data together for one download; you can choose Wi-Fi only, or set a daily mobile data limit, in Settings.

For downloads you start in the built-in browser, that site's cookies are sent back to the same site, so downloads that need you to be signed in keep working. They are kept in memory only and are not saved by FetchKosh. Google Drive links are downloaded directly from Google Drive in the same way.

The built-in browser blocks requests to known advertising servers using a list that is built into the app. This check happens on your phone; nothing about the pages you visit is sent anywhere.

Copied links (while the app is open)

When you open FetchKosh, it checks whether the text you copied is a download link, so it can offer to download it. The check happens only on your phone; the copied text is not saved or sent anywhere.

Speed test (only when you start it)

The speed test downloads test data from Cloudflare's speed test server (speed.cloudflare.com) for about 10 seconds, over Wi-Fi and, if allowed in Settings, mobile data. Cloudflare receives your IP address and the request, like any website; FetchKosh sends nothing else. The result is shown only on your phone and is not saved or shared. Mobile data used by the test counts toward your daily limit. Cloudflare's privacy policy.

Watch on TV (only when you turn it on)

When "Share with TV" is on, your phone announces itself on your home Wi-Fi with its device name, so FetchKosh on your Android TV can find it. A TV can connect only after you approve it with the code shown on the TV, or after you scan the QR code on that TV with your phone.

The TV then plays the videos, music and photos you chose, straight from your phone over your Wi-Fi. Other files you choose are copied to the TV only when you pick them on the TV. Nothing goes over the internet, and nothing is sent to us.

So your TVs still recognise this phone after FetchKosh is reinstalled, the phone gives them an ID made from the ID Android gives FetchKosh on this phone. It is sent only to TVs on your Wi-Fi.

Scanning the TV's code (only when you use it)

Google's code scanner, part of Google Play services, reads the QR code. The camera picture stays with Google Play services; FetchKosh receives only the text of the code and has no access to your camera. Google may collect diagnostic information about the scanner under Google's privacy policy.

Phone 5G for your PC (only when you turn it on)

FetchKosh on your own Windows PC can use your phone's mobile data as one more connection for its downloads, like a hotspot used by one app. The phone announces itself on your Wi-Fi so the PC can find it, and a PC can connect only after you approve it with a code. The PC's download traffic passes through your phone to the websites it downloads from; secure (HTTPS) connections stay encrypted end to end, so the phone cannot read them. FetchKosh counts how much data your PC used today so your daily limit applies. You can remove an allowed PC at any time.

Send to PC and Send to phone (only when you use them)

  • Only the files and links you choose are sent, straight between your phone and your own Windows PC over your home Wi-Fi. They are not uploaded to the internet, to us or to anyone else, and no mobile data is used.
  • Only PCs you approved with a code can send files to the phone or receive them.
  • Files from your PC are saved in Download/FetchKosh on the phone. A link you send with "Download on PC" is downloaded by your PC from that website.

Sharing (only when you tap it)

"Share how fast it was" makes a picture of your download's speed on your phone. "Send to PC" in the "Get FetchKosh for Windows" card shares the download page's address. Both go only to the app you pick in Android's share menu.

Android TV app

On a TV, FetchKosh shows a code or QR code so you can connect your phone or PC, and plays the files they share over your home Wi-Fi. It keeps the list of phones and PCs you connected, and saves the files you choose to keep in Download/FetchKosh or in a folder you pick (such as a USB pen drive). Nothing is sent over the internet or to us.

Windows app

What stays on your PC

  • The files you download, in the download folder you choose.
  • Your settings, download list and the phones and TVs you connected, in %APPDATA%\FetchKosh.

Internet connections

  • Downloads: the websites of the links you add, over every connection your PC has (and your phone's mobile data if you turned that on).
  • Updates: FetchKosh asks GitHub (api.github.com) whether a new version is out when it starts and every few hours. With "Update automatically" on, it downloads the new installer from GitHub. GitHub receives your IP address, like any website. GitHub's privacy statement.
  • Speed test (only when you start it): test data from speed.cloudflare.com, as on the phone.
  • Get Android app: the button and QR code open the FetchKosh page on Google Play.

Copied links

With "Catch copied download links" on (you can turn it off in Settings), FetchKosh checks whether text you copy is a download link and offers to download it. The check happens on your PC; nothing is saved or sent.

Your phone and TV

Phone 5G, Send to phone and Watch on TV work over your home Wi-Fi only, with the same approval codes and rules as described for the phone app above. A shared folder is played by your TV straight from your PC; nothing is uploaded.

Game Boost (only when you turn it on)

Game Boost speeds up Steam and Epic Games downloads. When you switch a launcher on, FetchKosh adds a few lines to Windows' hosts file so the launcher sends its game downloads to FetchKosh on your PC; a small helper, set up once with your permission, removes those lines when FetchKosh closes. FetchKosh then fetches the game's pieces from Steam's or Epic's own servers over your connections; secure (HTTPS) connections stay encrypted end to end. To choose the fastest servers, it asks Steam's public server list which servers are near each of your connections. "Find my best Steam region" downloads a few public test pieces from Steam's servers, only after you press Start, and reads Steam's own settings and log files on your PC to show your current region; it never changes them. None of this is sent to us.

Starting with Windows

With "Start with Windows" on, FetchKosh starts when you sign in to Windows (through a Task Scheduler task for your user only), so unfinished downloads can continue.

Browser extension

The FetchKosh extension hands downloads from your browser to FetchKosh on the same PC. To do that it asks the browser for permission to see downloads, add a right-click menu item, keep its settings, and read the cookies of the site a download comes from.

  • It sends the download's address, the page it came from and that site's cookies only to FetchKosh on your own PC (at 127.0.0.1), so downloads that need you to be signed in work. Nothing is sent anywhere else.
  • The button the extension adds on some pages sends only that page's address, without cookies.
  • FetchKosh on your PC accepts these requests only from the extension, not from websites.

This website

fetchkosh.pages.dev is hosted by Cloudflare Pages. It sets no cookies and has no analytics or tracking. Cloudflare handles the connection and may keep standard technical logs (such as IP addresses) to run and protect the service. The page's fonts are loaded from Google Fonts, so your browser connects to Google for them. The download buttons take you to Google Play and to GitHub, where the Windows installer is stored.

Android permissions

  • Internet, network state and changing network state: to download over Wi-Fi and mobile data at the same time.
  • Notifications: to show download progress, finished downloads, and when sharing with your TV or PC is on.
  • Foreground service (data sync and connected device): to keep downloads, TV sharing and Phone 5G for your PC running while you use other apps.
  • Wake lock: so a running download doesn't stop when the screen turns off.
  • Run at startup: to continue downloads you started after the phone restarts.
  • Storage (Android 9 only, for example older TVs): to save files in Download/FetchKosh. Android 10 and newer don't need it, so FetchKosh doesn't ask for it there.

FetchKosh does not ask for your contacts, location, camera or microphone, or for access to all your files. Files are shared with your TV only after you pick them with Android's own file picker.

Removing your data

Everything FetchKosh keeps is on your own devices, so you remove it there:

  • Phone and TV: uninstalling FetchKosh removes the download list, settings and allowed devices. Downloaded files stay in your Downloads folder until you delete them.
  • Windows: uninstall FetchKosh from Settings → Apps. To also remove your settings and device list, delete the %APPDATA%\FetchKosh folder. Downloaded files stay where you saved them.
  • Allowed devices: you can remove a TV or PC in the app at any time.

Since we hold no data about you, there is nothing for us to delete; you can still write to us with any question.

Children

FetchKosh is not directed at children under 13.

Changes

When this policy changes, the new version is posted on this page with a new date. If a change affects what FetchKosh collects, the app's listing on Google Play is updated at the same time.
